Common questions

How do I restore a subscription?
Open the app, go to Ruth+, and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account you originally subscribed with.

How do I cancel?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and select With Ruth. You can also reach this from Settings inside the app.

How do I delete my account?
Open Settings inside the app and choose Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile and all of your journal entries.

Where are my journal entries stored?
Your entries are stored privately in your account and are never shown to anyone else. See the Privacy Policy for details.
